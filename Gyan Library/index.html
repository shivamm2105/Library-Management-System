Why Choose Gyan Library?

24×7 Study Environment

Study at any hour that suits you. Our doors are open around the clock to support your learning journey.

High-Speed WiFi Access

Enjoy seamless connectivity with high-speed fiber internet throughout the library campus.

Academic Books

Competitive Exams

Exhaustive study materials for UPSC, SSC, Banking, JEE, NEET, and other competitive examinations.

CCTV Security

24/7 CCTV surveillance and security guards on-site, ensuring a safe study space for everyone.

Neet & Clean Washrooms

Regularly maintained and sanitized washrooms for a hygienic study environment.

Daily Newspapers

Stay updated with leading national and international newspapers, magazines, and periodicals.

Dedicated Study Cabins
